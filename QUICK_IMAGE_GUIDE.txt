========================================
  QUICK GUIDE: Add Your Project Images
========================================

STEP 1: Copy Your Images from Desktop
--------------------------------------

Open Terminal and run these commands (one at a time):

# Example: Copy an image from Desktop and rename it
cp ~/Desktop/your-donna-ai-image.jpg ~/Desktop/portfolio_website/public/projects/project1.jpg
cp ~/Desktop/your-analytics-image.png ~/Desktop/portfolio_website/public/projects/project2.png
cp ~/Desktop/your-social-image.jpg ~/Desktop/portfolio_website/public/projects/project3.jpg

Replace "your-donna-ai-image.jpg" with your actual filename!


OR Using Finder (Easier for most people):
------------------------------------------

1. Open TWO Finder windows:
   - Window 1: Your Desktop (where your images are)
   - Window 2: Navigate to Desktop/portfolio_website/public/projects/

2. Drag and drop your images into the projects folder

3. Rename them to:
   project1.jpg (for Donna AI)
   project2.jpg (for Analytics Dashboard)
   project3.jpg (etc.)


STEP 2: Images Will Show Automatically!
----------------------------------------

✅ Code is already updated to use local images
✅ Just refresh your browser: http://localhost:3000
✅ Your images should appear in the Projects section!


IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
-------------------
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Size: Around 800x500 pixels (recommended)
- Keep under 500KB for fast loading
